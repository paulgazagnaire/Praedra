Lore
THE WORLD
A city-world somewhat larger than most with no wild ground left: one city covers the surface in planned districts of towers and office grids, and the climate is run from engines under the crust. The old seas survive only as a few regulated basins between the districts, and there is almost no ice. The Board sits in the old Parliament hall at the centre of the oldest district.
FROM SPACE
A solid gold-and-white grid of lights with no dark side; by day the districts read gold and pale grey round a few dark basins. An orbital ring girdles the equator, white with a red band, and four elevator cables rise from the ground to stations high above it.
PEOPLE
About 900 billion hands of every grade, from the Board families in the central towers to the cleaners who work them at night.
MOONS
Four small moons: the first, the Tabularium, hollowed out and plated over as the vault of the debt books, lit at every window and ringed by guard platforms; then a cratered grey rock, a small captured lump and a second cratered rock, which anchor the traffic beacons.
