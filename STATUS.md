**TITAN + BIG asteroids + gravity (new):**
- **Every procedural seed carries exactly one TITAN** (r 1035–1440, ~5× a BIG) **plus 1–3 BIG asteroids** (r 220–380, vs 26–90 for ordinary rocks), placed in that order so clusters/lanes flow around them; arena grown 4400×3200 → 8000×5600 and match timer 300s → 360s to give titan-scale maps transit room (rock counts rescaled). The titan roams anywhere — including cut by the arena boundary — but always keeps ≥65% of its disc in the playable zone (deterministic disc sampler), so it always shapes the fight.
- Asteroid outlines: 18–160 vertices scaling with radius (three harmonic bands — continent lobes, ridges, crater-scale detail — plus fine grit); detail rank follows size rank, the titan carries 130+. The outline is also the **contact surface**: rock-rock resting/collision, hull bonks, accretion support and warhead strikes resolve on the interpolated contour (`contourR`), so accretion piles hug the lumps instead of revealing an invisible bounding circle. LOS/rays/ranges stay circular on r.
- Velocity-scaled gravity on projectiles: deflection is naturally ~g·L/v² (torpedoes/bombs at ~420 px/s curl visibly through wells; the 2400 px/s DESTROYER railgun slug's real sagitta is ~1px and it stays hitscan — the battleship's heavy-rail slug is now a real projectile and skips gravity entirely). Destroyer rail traces render with that physical sagitta boosted `gravity.slugBendVisual`× (render-only) so the speed hierarchy reads on screen.
- Wells are **bounded**: pull fades to zero at `wellReach`×radius (the titan's raw r³ well would otherwise act map-wide). Within a well, EVERYTHING responds: the old 650px wake-shell is gone and `rockWake` sits at 1.2 px/s² (just above the drag/settle stall boundary), so whole fields creep, grey rocks visibly fall, and BIG asteroids themselves slide into the titan over minutes. Piles rest once supported; the titan (never out-massed) stays the fixed anchor.
- **Gravity**: rocks with r ≥ 100 (the BIGs + their first-gen fragments) are wells with mass r³, accel `G·r³/(d²+(0.5r)²)`, clamped/cutoff. Ships drift and must burn against it (heavies near a monster rock genuinely struggle); debris curls into the wells and **accretes**; every settled rock a well reaches wakes (unless supported down-well or below the wake threshold) — nothing in a well is exempt; torpedoes/bombs bend course but keep design speed. Cinematic constant, deterministic (no RNG). Perf companions for whole-field creep: a per-tick mover bucket-grid (the flat movers scan went O(movers²)), a cached pair-contact contour (`pairContact`), staggered collision checks for sub-30px/s creepers, and amortized wake evaluation (every 3rd tick per rock).
- **Tuning**: gravity slider ×0–3 in the fleet-setup screen AND a live slider in the top bar (mutates `match.config.gravity.G` mid-match; restart keeps the tuned value). `__praedra.setGravity(mult)` for automation.
- Renderer: massive rocks get a cratered look + faint field-contour rings (strong-pull and well-edge).
- Perf: spatial grid now holds settled rocks only (movers scanned as a short list); grid rebuilds only on settle/wake/split flips. Small-fleet match ~700µs/tick at titan scale (harness budget is per-tick now — titan-scale hunts are legitimately long in sim-seconds).
- Anti-entombment set (found by adversarial batteries): ships shove settled pebbles (r < `collision.pushableRockRadius`) aside instead of being walled in by accretion shells; pull on a ship is capped at `gravity.shipEscapeCap` × its own thrust accel (wells threaten, never imprison — keeps the ×3 slider playable); settled rocks are true terrain in rock-rock collisions (accreting pebbles no longer walk the titan across the map — anchors verified at 0.0px drift over 90s); the autopilot stall-breaker commits to one sidestep side per stall episode (picked away from the pinning rock) instead of dithering.
- Titan-scale navigation (found by tracing full-match stalls): the autopilot has **gravity feed-forward** (the burn buys the velocity change AND the hover — station-keeping in a well now costs visible throttle, which feeds the detection plume rules); hunting fleets **route around the titan's flank** instead of pathing through its well (`routeAround`, tangent waypoints at r+900); titan-scale rocks are rim-followed (tangent-bug) when a path must cross them; the `bigRockAvoidPad` is a transit margin, not a wall — it relaxes for a rock when the ship's goal legitimately sits at that rock's rim (ordered points and cover spots near bigs are reachable again).

**Sim (DOM-free, deterministic, force-resolving):**
- **BATTLESHIP class:** a fifth ship class — a ponderous radius-78 gun platform (cost 15, hp 620, cruise 28, signature 4200 = largest in the game). Mounts a **heavy railgun**, now a true PROJECTILE weapon (kinetic rework): 3 independent turrets (`ship.turrets = [{ang, cool, load, lost}]`) with **WWII firing arcs** (`arcCenters` [0, 0, π] ± `arcHalfWidth` 2.36 — the bow pair is blind astern, the aft turret blind over the bow, full broadside only on the beam; out-of-arc mounts train to their limit stop and wait), **5 s per-turret reload**, a **visible 1.2 s load cycle** (rails charge whenever the solution holds, incl. during cooldown; a broken solution dumps the charge only after a 0.25 s grace so a burning hull's nav yaws don't starve the guns), slow slew (**0.35 rad/s**), aim tolerance, dead zone 220, huge but detection-bounded range 3200 (vs railgun 700). Each shot launches a real slug (`state.slugs`, 2400 px/s, no gravity, not PD-interceptable) along the barrel; slugs **PIERCE trackable-class hulls** (one uniform evasion×speed roll per hull, any team — friendly fire preserved — and the slug flies on) and are **stopped dead by asteroids**. Hard class gate (a slug overpenetrates bombers/interceptors without fuzing — never damages them) and a speed gate (a frigate at cruise 85 is untrackable; a slowed one is very hittable). The only RNG is the per-hull in-flight hit roll (slug-order then along-segment order → deterministic). `aiBattleship` now runs an **engagement-aware standoff** (holds at its firing range out to `heavyRail.standoffFrac`×maxRange, closes to `heavyRail.standoffMin` to acquire when it has no shot, and — mirroring `aiDestroyer`'s noLos pattern — flags the rock plugging its lane for the turrets to demolish while it advances), creeps away from light swarms, and blasts cover; turrets run in every AI/order path. A **battleship-only gravity-well skirt** (`ai.bbWellSkirtFrac`) routes the ponderous hull around titan/BIG wells in its auto role AI — without it the turnMax-0.14 ship was dragged into wells and ground to death before firing. Deliberately fragile to bomb AOE (fully in the blast) yet big enough that a 5+ bomber wave immolates itself (self-splash) — a pair fails, a squad gets it. Spawn pitch widened to 2.4×largest-radius so the big hull doesn't spawn interpenetrating. Fleet-setup gains a `BB` row (⬢, heavies-first). See `docs/SIM_CONTRACT.md` §Heavy railgun. **Real-match effectiveness TUNED (multi-BB fleets now delete big ships); lone-BB balance caveat in Known items.**
- **Friendly fire universal**: railgun/gatling hit the first hull on the firing ray regardless of team; torpedoes contact-detonate on any non-owner hull; all AOE (bombs, chains, torpedo interceptions) hits everyone.
- **Asteroids always destructible**: rock HP 80×(r/60)²; railgun ×2.2 and torpedo ×2.5 demolition coupling; destroyed rocks split into 4 polygonal, momentum-carrying, spinning fragments (bounded cascade, cap 500); debris drifts (drag 0.35), collides momentum-conservingly, and shreds hulls on impact.
- **Detection**: per-class signature (destroyer 2800 / frigate 1700 / bomber 900 / interceptor 750) × thrust-plume modulation (0.6 coasting → 1.2 full burn), LOS required, team-shared picture (state.detA/detB), last-seen memory. ALL targeting, threats, PD ship-tracking, and commit waves are detection-gated; blind ships hunt toward last contact.
- **Cover demolition AI**: destroyers (railgun) and frigates (torpedoes) fire on rocks that hide a recently-seen ghost or block a shot at a detected target — no hesitation (1.2 s).
- **Class pathing**: avoidance margins scale with hull (destroyer 2.2×); capitals sample sideways offsets and drift toward open ground with a clean firing lane. **Field detour + lane clearing (new):** on the AUTO/hunt path a capital measures corridor clutter and *rounds* a compact, clutter-heavy field (`routeAround` two-leg flank scoring, staggered+cached per ship) instead of grinding through it; while transiting (hunt AND ordered move/attackmove/hold) it *clears blockers out of its lane* (`clearTransitLane`: destroyer railguns, frigate torpedoes, battleship turrets demolish the most-dead-ahead rock), combat-priority-gated (a detected enemy in weapon range owns the gun) with a debris-safety standoff so fragments settle before they can reach the shooter. Autopilot no longer weaves around harmless pushable pebbles (the hull shoves them), so a slow capital isn't pinned in its own shatter-debris. New `ai.*` knobs: `clutterDetour*`, `rockClear*` (AI-internal — see SIM_CONTRACT §Key CONFIG fields).
- **Flanking**: 50% of commit waves hook wide left/right before turning in.
- **Player orders**: `Praedra.issueOrder` — move / attackmove / attack / attackrock / hold / auto, per-class execution, opportunistic weapons-free fire, completed orders drop to Hold (PRD §9 semantics).

## Known items / notes
- Balance: the flip-gate question is parked (see LESSONS.md and git history for the full record); current AI-vs-AI win rates lean RAILGUN at most densities. The sandbox direction (detection, FF, demolition) reshuffled balance — retune when the flip work resumes. The bigger arena + gravity reshuffle it further; ~10% of armada matches resolve on the timer (both fleets camped in the mutual LOS shadow of a BIG asteroid, or the endgame interceptor-vs-capital slog — scored correctly either way). Cutting that further is hunt-AI tuning; revisit with the flip retune.
- A fully-static scenario where a rock permanently blocks the only sightline is a stalemate by design (never-detected targets can't be engaged); irrelevant in real matches (documented in SIM_CONTRACT).
- `destructibleAsteroids` config key is ignored (kept for compat).
- **Battleship real-match effectiveness TUNED (AI-positioning pass); deep balance still pending the flip retune.** A 96-match battery (2 BB fleets × RAILGUN/BALANCED/SWARM × seeds 1-8 × densities 0.3/0.6) diagnosed the old "near-useless" behaviour as, in order: (1) the slow hull dragged into the titan/BIG gravity **wells and ground to death** (`by:'rock'`) in the hunt phase before firing (dominant killer — with gravity OFF the same seeds survive and deal 450-960); (2) parking at maxRange×0.85 (~2720px) **past terrain LOS** with an idle gun even when a capital was detected but rock-blocked. Fixes (all config-driven, in `aiBattleship`, ordered transit untouched → lane-clear tests unaffected): an **engagement-aware standoff** (`heavyRail.standoffFrac`/`standoffMin`) that holds at its firing range and closes-to-acquire + demolishes the plugging rock (mirrors `aiDestroyer`'s noLos) instead of parking dark; and a **gravity-well skirt** (`ai.bbWellSkirtFrac`) that rounds titan/BIG wells (its 3200px guns still shell across them). Result vs baseline (median BB dmg vs capital fleets 0 → 21.5; % matches BB deals >0 dmg 48% → 58%): the **two-BB fleet is transformed** (vs RAILGUN: median BB dmg 15 → 285, survival 41% → 72%, fleet win 56% → 81%; vs BALANCED median 19 → 95), and win-rates are in the intended band (RAILGUN 66%, BALANCED 66% ≤75, SWARM 84% — it still generally beats SWARM rather than losing to it, a residual imbalance). **Caveat: the LONE battleship (BB1 fleet) still dies to terrain/gravity entombment in ~50-60% of seeds** — a turnMax-0.14 / cruise-28 hull on titan-scale gravity maps trades survival against engagement and positioning tweaks (skirt, avoidMult, hunt-speed all swept) can't push its median past the wall, so the combined median (21.5) stays under the ≥150 goal even though multi-BB fleets clear it. Favoured "deletes big ships, dies to bombers/terrain" per the tuning brief. Remaining work for the flip retune: lone-BB survivability (likely needs hull/collision/gravity levers beyond AI positioning), the mild SWARM over-performance, and full-armada balance. Durability vs bombers is still tuned only in the isolated pinned-corner case.
