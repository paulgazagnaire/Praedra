# LESSONS

- **There is a first-mover/side bias in mirror matches** (team A wins ~60% of RAILGUN mirrors regardless of doctrine — A's ships tick/fire first each frame and spawn-side terrain varies). NEVER read an AI change from a single orientation: run v2-as-A AND v2-as-B and sum wins across both.
- **Station-keeping drift-chase was the battleship's hidden fire-uptime killer.** The autopilot burned to cancel ANY residual drift ≥3 px/s; pointing the burn takes a full hull rotation, which at turnMax 0.14 is ~20s of spinning turret arcs off-target per correction. Accepting ≤12 px/s of drift for slow hulls took demolition fire uptime from ~10% to continuous. Trace the FULL control loop (nav → hull → mounts) before blaming the weapon layer.
- **Demolishing big rocks is self-harm without radius-scaled standoff.** An r-320 rock splits into r-166 children that shred the shooter at any drift speed (damage ∝ r²·v — travel distance is speed-bound, lethality is not). Fragment TRAVEL is size-independent; fragment DANGER is not.
- **A dead zone is only a dead zone against the weapon that has one.** Wolfpack dives are safe vs battleships (heavyRail.minRange 420) and suicide vs destroyers (railgun minRange 150, range 700). Gate aggressive doctrine on the VICTIM's weapon envelope, not on 'capital'.
- **In furballs, spacing beats dodging.** Bomb salvos + AOE chains kill clumps; doctrine that concentrates lights (guard your bombers!) must pair with splash-spread (≥110px between friendly lights) or it feeds the enemy's salvos. Also: react to the SHOT (inbound bomb ⇒ jink), not just the shooter.
- **Synchronized-volley holds must be cheap.** A 3s hold for PD-saturation sync cost 20-30% launch volume with NO measured interception-rate gain (same-bearing sync doesn't beat slot-capped PD; bearing diversity does). Measure the mechanism you think you're buying.
- **Self-defense overrides doctrine priorities.** 'Kill their bombers first' made v2 interceptors fly past enemy interceptors at guns range and doubled gatling deaths. Any target-priority list needs a 'someone is shooting ME right now' escape hatch.
- **For interceptable weapons, CLOSER beats everything.** Three separate torpedo-targeting 'improvements' (PD-shadow weight 80, fleet-focus bonus -400, 1.2-3s volley holds) each traded launch distance or volume for concentration/efficiency — and each LOST, because +100px of flight is another PD volley window plus another jink window. Median launch range was the single most predictive stat (v2 615 vs v1 478 = losing; parity = winning). Focus and PD-thinness earn tie-break weights only.
- **Ablate before you add.** The rail mirror sat at 45-49% through three rounds of added features; a 4-run config-knob ablation (EMCON/cohesion/volley off) took 15 minutes and showed the base build (with the focus tie-break) already at 60% — the fix was a constant, not a feature. Config-gate every doctrine behavior for exactly this reason.
- **Formation geometry needs a hull-clearance check.** The wolfpack's uncapped bearing fan assigned outer slots whose straight approach chord passed THROUGH the victim's hull (95px closest approach vs 94px combined radii) — the boats bounced off the battleship forever. Any 'spread around the target' scheme must cap the fan so chords clear the target.

- **Prior build's flip measured +0 (railgun won both densities).** Root cause read: turreted railgun hit anything with LOS, so cover didn't matter. This build's counter-mechanics (fixed-forward gun, min range, evasion gating, solid asteroids) exist for that reason — never regress them for "balance."
- **Never wait on wall-clock for a result.** Headless matches force-resolve at a hard tick cap and return immediately; batches are many fast bounded runs.
- **vm.runInNewContext makes top-level globals pathologically slow.** The sim must live inside one IIFE exposing only `Praedra`; the flat version cost 5× (10s/match dense → 2s). Profile before optimizing: my assumed hotspot (rock loops) was wrong, `LoadGlobalIC` was the real one.
- **Win rates alone are useless for diagnosis.** Damage-by-source + deaths-by-cause + launch-range stats found every real problem (gatling grind dominance, frigate point-blank torpedo executions, bombers stalling at v=2 against rock rims, bombs detonating on clutter mid-flight).
- **The fight migrates to open pockets.** Capitals can't sit inside rocks, so local density around the fight ≈ low regardless of global density. High `terrainDensity` must scatter sparse rocks EVERYWHERE (no clean pockets), or the flip variable never touches the actual battle. (sparseRockMax 44 exists for this.)
- **Interceptor anti-capital power is the flip-flattener, exactly as §5 warned.** A "dogpile" mode (stay in the dead zone, grind with gatling) made SWARM win everywhere; strafe-pass discipline + modest capitalMult is load-bearing. Related: torpedoes fired at interceptors are always wasted (they jink on threat → terminal hit ≈ 0) — the AI must not bait its magazine away on them; and the frigate must LOB from standoff (`torpedo.lobMinRange`), else it becomes a point-blank executioner inside the dense-map brawl, which is density-independent and kills the flip.
- **Autopilot stalls were the silent killer of the bomber pipeline.** Goals just past a rock rim are local traps; avoidance side-dither stalls ships at v≈20 in clutter. Waypoint-detour around the blocking rock's rim + per-rock side hysteresis + a stall-breaker fixed delivery. Watch bombers' bombs-fired stat any time nav code changes.
- **One knob at a time re-flattens the curve.** Every single-lever fix slid the whole win-rate curve up or down; the flip needs BOTH ends sharpened via terrain-gated channels (run length coupled to sightline availability, cover for staging, saturation timing), not flat power shifts.
- **Frigate-first targeting is the swarm's best strategy** (strip the torpedo platform + 3 PD slots, then the blinded destroyer is helpless per §5) and it is achievable mainly where the approach is covered — worth +5-10 pts of flip on its own. Destroyer-first wastes waves into the full escort complex.
- **The three structural walls that cap dense-end SWARM at ~40% (why bounded tuning could not produce the crossover):**
  1. *PD is terrain-immune by construction.* Its 140 px bubble sits inside the rock-free pocket that necessarily surrounds a navigating capital, so the A-side's #1 swarm-killer works identically at every density — and §5 forbids weakening it enough to change that (verified: every PD nerf, including the sanctioned pdSlots cut, collapses the open end before it fixes the dense end).
  2. *The swarm's delivery pays a clutter tax that rises with density* (bombs clip rocks, runs abort on LOS loss, transit cycles stretch), partially cancelling cover's protection. Pushing terrain density past ~0.9 makes SWARM WORSE (39/60 timer-outs at max clutter).
  3. *Timer force-resolve scoring by surviving fleet value structurally favors capitals* exactly in the grindy states dense terrain produces (at 0.9 with a 300 s timer: A wins 18 eliminations + 7 timers, B wins 14 eliminations + 1 timer).
- **Mechanic-change candidates for the human (each attacks one wall):** (a) PD reaction/track time ≈ 0.5 s before it may engage a newly-visible target — pop-outs from cover then beat PD in dense, open unchanged — my top pick; (b) bomber salvo release (2–3 bombs per trigger) to spike past the slot cap; (c) timer scoring by damage-dealt/hp-lost ratio instead of surviving fleet value; (d) a real torpedo arming distance replacing the `lobMinRange` AI hack.\n- **PD reaction gating protects launches, not launchers.** The envelope (reach < range + speed*reaction) is symmetric in terrain; only approach-survival is terrain-gated. Pairing it with strong tracked-interception ("strong when tracking, blind to pop-outs") is the right shape: `projectileKillChance` 0.7 held the open end while dense pop-outs leak.
- **Cover-point stability is a two-edged knob** (`ai.coverHoldTicks`): 0.5 s rescoring keeps open-map staging exposed (torpedo tax intact); 2 s holds double dense arrivals but shelter open bombers and flatten the flip to 0. This single confound silently inverted two whole tuning rounds — A/B config knobs, never hardcode behavior changes mid-sweep.
- **Timer length converts A-timer wins into B-eliminations at the dense end** (B led elims 24-18 at 0.9). The clock is a RAILGUN weapon; 300 s (PRD band max) is the honest setting.
- **Destructible asteroids currently favor the railgun side** — shattering cover opens torpedo/railgun lanes faster than debris hurts the capitals. The §8 "flip survives destructibles" requirement will need its own design attention once the static flip exists.
- **Salvo + sympathetic detonation works as intended** both ways: per-lane launch checks are mandatory (outer fan bombs clipping rocks chain-killed their own bombers), and tight clusters die to one tracked interception in the open.\n- **Detection reuses LOS, so "never seen" means "never engaged"** — fully static scenarios with a dead-center blocking rock stalemate by design. Scripted tests must place targets inside coasting visible range (signature × thrustMultMin) and off the rock axis.
- **vm scripts and the browser share the sim verbatim** — the RTS layer drives the same `issueOrder` API the tests exercise headless; UI work never touched the SIM block, which is what made parallel subagent work on one file safe.
